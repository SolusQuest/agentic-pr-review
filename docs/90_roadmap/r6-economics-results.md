# R6 economics conclusion and R7 evidence handoff

[Issue #281](https://github.com/SolusQuest/agentic-pr-review/issues/281) assesses the already executed R6 evidence under [master #267](https://github.com/SolusQuest/agentic-pr-review/issues/267). **Disposition: evidence insufficient for R6 exit or R7 release/default graduation.** The offline economics machinery and one bounded live restored continuation are demonstrated. The selected representative live schedules, a comparable complete cost pair, independently established model quality, and several operational behaviors are not. R7 design refinement may use this record and its open gates; it is not a promotion decision. This documentation task makes no provider call, release, default, or issue/milestone closure decision.

## Comparison and quality decision

The [C1 comparison contract](../10_workflow/r6-economics.md) requires each entire selected schedule to complete, full usage and monetary coverage, a common declared reference tariff, and compatible fixed workload/configuration factors before a descriptive cost difference is admissible. Neither 23-stage #280 campaign completed. #291 completed its selected two stages, but changed source, workload and schedule and has no matched complete partner. **There is no eligible pair and no observed cost-regression verdict.** The known totals above must not be subtracted or used to erase the first campaign's unknown-cost send. A formal promotion-blocking regression rule additionally lacks an execution-bound pre-confirmatory receipt; no threshold is inferred after seeing these runs.

The Sep 23 and #291 reports also show same-token all-miss repricing of $0.007248000 and $0.007400700, respectively. Each applies the miss rate to that campaign's **same** observed tokens. Neither is a measured cache-disabled control, provider savings, or an equivalent alternate execution. The Sep 22 counterfactual remains unknown. Provider cache keys and a supported cache-off/stateless control have not been evidenced; a fresh process, new session or `user_id` would not establish such a control. The fixed-reference hit/miss partitions are observed telemetry, not a causal cache benefit.

The [R5 live quality result](r5-evaluation-results.md) retained zero quality-eligible cases and no usable precision or recall despite its completed engineering delivery. #280's completed bootstrap did not have independent quality adjudication; #291's two zero-expected-defect synthetic cases do not establish general model quality, and native `Adjudicated`/zero-findings labels do not prove independent human confirmation. Quality and safety remain separate from successful SESSION restoration, observed cache hits, and low reference-priced totals.
